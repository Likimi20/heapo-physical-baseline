3. PV - AND WHY IT DOES NOT BIAS CATEGORY B
==============================================================================
  PV on the modellable, post-visit population:
    {False: np.int64(99), nan: np.int64(25), True: np.int64(21)}
  Absence is NOT 'no PV' - Unknown is its own level (parent trap 3).

  deficiency by PV level:
    PV       n=21   median deficiency   1522   decisive  90.5%
    Unknown  n=25   median deficiency   2016   decisive 100.0%
    no PV    n=99   median deficiency   1356   decisive  85.9%

  MEASURED CONCLUSION: Category B is computed from geometry, era, JAZ,
  residents and HDD. IT NEVER TOUCHES THE METER. A PV household's grid
  import under-states its true draw, but that number does not enter the
  deficiency at all. So PV DOES NOT BIAS CATEGORY B.

  PV bites only on meter-based checks. Here is the damage, on the level:
    PV       implied non-heating electricity: median   3679 kWh/yr  5-95%    -124 -   10501
    Unknown  implied non-heating electricity: median   6964 kWh/yr  5-95%    2344 -   18862
    no PV    implied non-heating electricity: median   5980 kWh/yr  5-95%    2544 -   16200
  If PV households sit LOWER, that is the invisible self-consumption, and
  it is a reason to keep them out of any meter-based comparison - never
  out of Category B.
