2. THE ASSUMED SHAPE - stated, not derived
==============================================================================
  d(ratio) = clip((ratio - 1.0)/(2.0 - 1.0), 0, 1) * d_max
  d_max in [0.05, 0.15], midpoint 0.10  (FHNW / BEYOND / Brudermueller)

  NOTHING SUPPLIED MAPS SIZING RATIO TO A PENALTY. The onset at 1.0 and

4. IS CAPACITY A ROUNDING ERROR OR A RIVAL?
==============================================================================
  ratio of the MID capacity scenario to each measured term:
    capacity / term_envelope  0.03
    capacity / term_dhw       0.13
    capacity / term_emitter   0.46

  households where the capacity scenario exceeds their DHW term : 52 of 160
  ...exceeds their ENVELOPE term                                : 17 of 160

  ANSWER: at FLEET level capacity is SMALL - 2.6% of the measured
  deficiency at the mid scenario, an eighth of the DHW term. It does not
  rival the envelope at any d_max in the supplied range.
  But it is CONCENTRATED, not uniform: half the fleet sits at or below
  its required capacity so the MEDIAN household carries zero, while a
  minority carry a term that beats their own envelope. A fleet share
  hides that - report it per household, never as a percentage.

==============================================================================
5. VALIDATION - and why it is CIRCULAR here, stated plainly
==============================================================================
  AUC of the capacity COST against the inspector's 'oversized' flag:
    0.793 [0.656, 0.911]  n 14 vs 136

  THIS IS NOT INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE. The cost is a monotone function of
  the sizing ratio, and P1/P4 already validated the RATIO against this
  same flag (0.772 and 0.786). The AUC here is the same fact restated,
  not a second confirmation. Reported so nobody quotes it as one.

  What WOULD be independent: disaggregating actual cycling frequency
  from the 15-minute meter and checking it rises with the sizing ratio.
  Not done - that is meter-based work and belongs on the A/C axis.
