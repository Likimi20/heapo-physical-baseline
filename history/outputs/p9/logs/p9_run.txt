  post2010      18    0.00582    0.00760     1.31

  THE PREDICTION: pre-1975 over post-2010, slope per m2
    physics predicts 3.38x
    the meter says   2.50x
    ratio of ratios  0.74
    measured ratio 95% CI [1.68, 3.45]

  A rank correlation cannot fail this test. A ratio can. If the
  measured multiple sits far from the predicted one, the U-values are
  wrong even when the ordering is right.
