6. CATEGORY C - compensating or compounding?
==============================================================================
  66 households carry both a setting fault and a confirmed excess.
  COMPENSATING: consumption sits near E_as_built - the house needs the
                heat it is getting. Turning the curve down will not stick.
  COMPOUNDING : consumption exceeds even E_as_built - deficient AND
                over-heated. Fix the setting first, it is free.

  {'compounding': np.int64(63), 'compensating': np.int64(3)}
  split at actual/E_as_built > 1.5, DECLARED AND ARBITRARY - E_as_built
  excludes appliance electricity, so the ratio is above 1 for everyone and
  no principled cut exists. See P4 section 3. This is a PLACEHOLDER.
