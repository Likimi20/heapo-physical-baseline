6. WITHIN-ERA VERSUS ACROSS-ERA RANKING
==============================================================================
  Miguel, 2026-09-10: the pre-1975 inconsistency matters less because we
  are comparing. That is TRUE WITHIN AN ERA and FALSE ACROSS ERAS.

  If every pre-1975 house is inflated by the same factor, their order
  among themselves is untouched - a common factor cannot reorder a list.
  But a single fleet-wide ranked list puts inflated pre-1975 houses above
  correctly-scaled modern ones that may waste more.

  So: RANK WITHIN ERA is safe under the P2 F1 defect.
      ONE FLEET-WIDE LIST is not, until the 1.59 is resolved.
