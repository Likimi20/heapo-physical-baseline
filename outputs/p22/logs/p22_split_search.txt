### 5. Variation by Construction Period

* **Swiss-specific historical component-area-to-floor-area ratios broken down by decade**: **not in the sources** (the uploaded Swiss sources provide reference building stock values and building count/ERA totals, but do not tabulate decade-by-decade \\(A_i / A_E\\) area ratios for single-family houses).
* **Observed Trends in German/TABULA Stock Data**:
  * Buildings become more thermally compact over time: the overall envelope-to-living-area ratio (\\(A_{\text{TH}} / A_{\text{living}}\\)) decreases from **2.72** (pre-1978) to **2.24** (1995–2009).
  * **Roof area relative to floor area** declines steadily: **0.83** (\\(\le 1978\\)) \\(\rightarrow\\) **0.75** (1979–1994) \\(\rightarrow\\) **0.70** (\\(\ge 1995\\)).
  * **Floor/cellar ceiling area relative to floor area** declines: **0.68** (\\(\le 1978\\)) \\(\rightarrow\\) **0.64** (1979–1994) \\(\rightarrow\\) **0.58** (\\(\ge 1995\\)).
  * **Wall area relative to floor area** remains relatively constant between **0.94 and 1.04**.
  * **Window area relative to floor area** remains constant at **0.22 to 0.23** across all construction eras.  
  * *Source*: *Scientific Report Germany: TABULA*, Table 22 (p. 45) and Table 25 (p. 52). (SFH, 2011/2012)
