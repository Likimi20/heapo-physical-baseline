### 3. Efficiency Loss with Operating Age (Long-Term Degradation)

#### **A. Brine Temperature Cooling in Geothermal Probes**
* **Observed Trend**: Brine return and flow temperatures circulating in geothermal boreholes drop gradually over operating years (*Unterkühlung*), which slightly lowers source temperature and reduces operational efficiency as the probe system ages [12].
  * **Source Title**: *Bericht Lebenszykluskosten von Wärmepumpen* (ZHAW, 2019) [12]
  * **Section / Page**: Section 4.1.3 & Abbildung 4.8, p. 14 [12]
  * **Building Type**: Residential single-family and multi-family buildings [2, 13]
  * **Year of Data**: 2019 (tracking ground probes installed between 1980 and 2020) [12]

#### **B. Multi-Year Field Performance Stability (FAWA Study)**
* **Air-Source Heat Pumps**: Relative seasonal performance (JAZ) remains stable in operating Years 2–5 (**1.01–1.02×** of Year 1 performance), followed by minor fluctuations in Years 6–9 (**0.96–0.98×** of Year 1 performance).
* **Ground-Source Heat Pumps**: Relative seasonal performance (JAZ) remains virtually constant across 9 operating years (**0.98–1.01×** of Year 1 performance), showing no significant long-term efficiency loss across nearly a decade of field operation.
  * **Source Title**: *FAWA – Feldanalyse von Wärmepumpen-Anlagen* (BFE, 2004) [14]
  * **Section / Page**: Section 10.1, p. 91–92, Abbildung 113 & 114
  * **Building Type**: Single-family and multi-family residential heat pumps (< 20 kWth capacity)
  * **Year of Data**: 2004 (monitoring period 1995–2003)
